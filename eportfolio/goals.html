05 / Career goals

Career goals

I want to work in hardware. I already have hands-on physical design experience, running RTL-to-GDS flows in Cadence Innovus, and I want to build on it in an internship next summer.

//What I’m looking for

I’m open to any hardware role, and I think that’s a strength at this stage. The areas I’m targeting for Summer 2027 internships:

ASIC

Design and verification

Writing RTL, building UVM testbenches and assertions, and chasing coverage.

PD

Physical design

Floorplanning, place and route, timing closure and DRC, which is what I do now at Silicon Jackets.

BOARD

Hardware test and PCB

Board-level design, bring-up and lab debug, like my EMG flex board and the Wheatstone build.

What ties these together is that I like seeing a design go from an idea to something physical that works, and I like being the person who finds out why it doesn’t work yet. An internship would show me how a real team does this at scale, with real schedules and real silicon.

  1. Fall 2026 · nowBuild and applyECE 2035, ECE 2040 and ECE 1100. Building my RISC-V CPU, starting physical design on the Vortex GPU at Silicon Jackets, and applying for Summer 2027 internships.

//Skills I’m working on

  • Timing. I can close timing on a small design. I want to get much better at reading timing reports and fixing hard paths on larger ones, like Vortex.
  • Verification depth. My calculator testbench reached 99.82% coverage. On the CPU I want to plan verification up front with a real test plan.
  • Lab skills. The Wheatstone build showed me how far real measurements can drift from simulation. I want more time with scopes, supplies and boards so I trust my own data.
  • Scripting. More Python and Tcl so the boring parts of a flow run themselves.
